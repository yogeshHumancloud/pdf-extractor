📍 Coordinate Capture Helper

📋 Instructions:

  1. Open your React app (http://localhost:3000)
  2. Upload the PDF and rules.json files
  3. Turn on "Selection Mode"
  4. For each field listed below, draw a box on the PDF
  5. Copy the coordinates from the console (they appear like: pdfCoords: {x: 28.33, y: 812.00, ...})
  6. Paste them into this form and click "Add Field"
  7. After all fields are captured, click "Generate JSON"
  8. Copy the generated JSON and save it as your updated rules file
